## 📖 Overview  
This repository contains **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** projects on:  

1️⃣ **Hotel Booking Data**:  
- Analyzing hotel booking trends, cancellation rates, and customer preferences.  
- Investigating seasonal booking trends and revenue impact.  

2️⃣ **Stock Market Data**:  
- Examining stock price movements, volatility, and trends.  
- Analyzing stock market patterns using **time-series analysis**.  

📊 **Insights Derived**:  
✔ Customer behavior & peak booking seasons in hotels.  
✔ Market trends, price fluctuations, and stock volatility analysis.  

---

## 🎯 Motivation  
EDA is crucial in **understanding datasets** before applying predictive models. This project aims to:  
✔ Discover patterns in hotel bookings & stock data.  
✔ Identify factors influencing **booking cancellations & stock fluctuations**.  
✔ Help businesses make **data-driven decisions**.  

---

## ⚙️ Technical Aspect  
1️⃣ **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**  
- Handling missing values  
- Encoding categorical variables  
- Converting date-time formats  

2️⃣ **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
- **Visualizing trends & correlations** using histograms, boxplots, and scatter plots  
- **Time-series analysis** for stock price movements  
- **Correlation heatmaps** to identify key factors  

3️⃣ **Insights & Findings**  
- **Hotel Bookings**: Peak seasons, cancellation factors, and revenue impact  
- **Stock Market**: Trend analysis, moving averages, and market fluctuations
